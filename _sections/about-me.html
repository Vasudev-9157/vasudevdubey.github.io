I work with ripples in spacetime — gravitational waves from crashing stars and black holes. Most days it’s less “Einstein at the chalkboard” and more “why won’t this script converge?” Still, I love the chase. I get to combine physics, math, and computing in a way that keeps me curious.
When I’m not wrestling with data, count me in for the occasional 100-meter sprint in the morning, evening walks or late night jamming.
